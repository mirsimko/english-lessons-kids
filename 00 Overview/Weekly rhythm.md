# Weekly rhythm

**One new sound per day, then repeat the whole set the following week.**

- **Week 1:** Days 1–6 introduce one new sound each. Day 7 is a relaxed review (songs, favourite games).
- **Week 2:** Go through the same six sounds again in the same order. The older child starts or extends oral blending.

Every session:

1. Quick review of earlier sounds (actions + songs) – 1–2 minutes.
2. Today's sound: story → action → song.
3. Activity (pick one or two from the sound note).
4. Older child: letter recognition / formation / blending.
5. Finish with a favourite song.
