# Age differences: around 2 vs around 4

| | Around 2 years | Around 4 years |
|---|---|---|
| Length | ~5 minutes, woven into play | 10–15 minutes, a small routine |
| Focus | Hearing the sound, copying the action, singing | Sound + action + recognising the letter |
| Writing | None – sensory tracing only (sand, play-dough, bath letters) | Air-writing → tracing → crayon |
| Games | Find-the-toy, action copy, songs | I spy, sound sorting, flashcard flash-and-act |
| Blending | Not expected | Oral blending of simple 2–3 sound words |
| Success looks like | Copying the action and making the sound | Knows the sounds of the group, blends a few words |

Tips for teaching both together:

- Do the story, action and song together; then give the older child a few extra minutes for letters and blending.
- Let the younger child "help" by doing the actions while the older child reads or blends.
- Follow the toddler's attention – it's fine if they wander off.
