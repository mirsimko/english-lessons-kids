# Approach

Jolly Phonics teaches the 42 letter sounds of English in 7 groups. Each sound comes with:

1. **A story** – a tiny scene that features the sound (a snake hissing in the grass for /s/).
2. **An action** – a hand or body movement the child does while saying the sound.
3. **A song** – a short tune that repeats the sound.

Principles for this vault:

- **Sounds first, letter names later.** Say /s/ ("sss"), not "ess".
- **Short and playful.** Stop while it's still fun.
- **Repetition wins.** Review earlier sounds every day.
- **Multi-sensory.** Move, sing, trace in sand, squish play-dough.
- **Praise effort**, not correctness.

The five core skills (for the older child, introduced gradually): learning the letter sounds, letter formation, blending, identifying sounds in words, and tricky words.
