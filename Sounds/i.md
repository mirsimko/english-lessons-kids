# /i/

← [[t]] · [[Group 1]] · [[p]] →

## Story
A little mouse with twitchy whiskers is spotted by the ink bottle.

## Action
Wiggle your fingers at the end of your nose like whiskers, saying i, i, i.

## Song idea
Sing "Inky the mouse, i, i, i…"

## Activities – around 2 years
- Pretend to be mice with wiggly whiskers.
- Hide a toy mouse and squeak i-i-i when it's found.

## Activities – around 4 years
- Find /i/: insect, igloo, ink.
- Write 'i' with a dot on top.

## Blending words
**i-t** → it, **s-i-t** → sit, **t-i-p** (after /p/), **p-i-n** (after /p/, /n/).
