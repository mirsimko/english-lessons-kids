# /e/

← [[c-k]] · [[Group 2]] · [[h]] →

## Story
Eggs are cracked into a pan for breakfast: e, e, e.

## Action
Pretend to tap an egg on the side of a pan and crack it, saying e, e, e.

## Song idea
Sing "Crack the egg into the pan, e, e, e…"

## Activities – around 2 years
- Crack plastic eggs open (hide small toys inside).
- Pretend-cook eggs in a toy kitchen.

## Activities – around 4 years
- Find /e/: egg, elephant, elbow.
- Write 'e': across, then round.

## Blending words
**p-e-n** → pen, **n-e-t** → net, **t-e-n** → ten, **p-e-t** → pet, **r-e-d** (after /r/, /d/), **h-e-n** (after /h/).
