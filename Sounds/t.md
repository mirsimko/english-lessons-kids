# /t/

← [[a]] · [[Group 1]] · [[i]] →

## Story
Watching a tennis match, heads turn side to side as the ball goes t, t, t.

## Action
Turn your head from side to side as if watching tennis, saying t, t, t.

## Song idea
Sing "Watching tennis, t, t, t…"

## Activities – around 2 years
- Roll a ball back and forth and say 't' on every catch.
- Turn heads side to side together, giggling.

## Activities – around 4 years
- I spy /t/: teddy, tap, table.
- Write 't': down, then across.
- Blending game: say s-a-t slowly, child says the word.

## Blending words
**a-t** → at, **s-a-t** → sat, **t-a-p** (after /p/).
