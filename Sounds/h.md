# /h/

← [[e]] · [[Group 2]] · [[r]] →

## Story
After running fast, everyone stops and pants: h, h, h.

## Action
Hold your hand in front of your mouth as if panting, saying h, h, h.

## Song idea
Sing "Hop, hop, hop, and pant, h, h, h…"

## Activities – around 2 years
- Run around, stop, and pant together.
- Hop like bunnies and pant.

## Activities – around 4 years
- I spy /h/: hat, hen, hand.
- Write 'h': tall down, up and over.

## Blending words
**h-a-t** → hat, **h-e-n** → hen, **h-i-t** → hit, **h-i-p** → hip, **h-a-s** → has.
