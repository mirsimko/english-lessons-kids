# /r/

← [[h]] · [[Group 2]] · [[m]] →

## Story
A puppy grabs a rag and shakes it, growling rrrr.

## Action
Pretend to be a puppy holding a rag, shaking your head, saying rrrrr.

## Song idea
Sing "The dog with the rag, rrrr…"

## Activities – around 2 years
- Play tug with a soft cloth and growl rrrr.
- Crawl like puppies.

## Activities – around 4 years
- Find /r/: rabbit, rain, red.
- Write 'r': down, up and a little hook.

## Blending words
**r-a-t** → rat, **r-e-d** → red, **r-i-p** → rip, **r-a-n** → ran, **t-r-a-p** → trap.
