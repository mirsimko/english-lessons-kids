# /s/

[[Group 1]] · [[a]] →

## Story
A snake slithers through the grass and hisses at a family out for a walk.

## Action
Weave your hand like a snake making an 's' shape, saying ssssss.

## Song idea
Sing "The snake is in the grass, sss, sss…" to a simple tune.

## Activities – around 2 years
- Hide toy snakes (or a sock 'snake') around the room and find them, hissing each time.
- Draw a big 's' in a sand tray or with a finger in flour.
- Make a play-dough snake and hiss at it.

## Activities – around 4 years
- I spy something that starts with /s/ (sock, spoon, sun).
- Air-write 's', trace it, then write it with a crayon.
- Sort pictures: starts with /s/ or not?

## Blending words
No blending yet – this is the first sound. Listen for /s/ at the start of words: sun, sock, sand.
