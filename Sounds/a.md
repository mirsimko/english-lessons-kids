# /a/

← [[s]] · [[Group 1]] · [[t]] →

## Story
Ants crawl up someone's arm during a picnic.

## Action
Wiggle your fingers up your arm like ants, saying a, a, a.

## Song idea
Sing "Ants on my arm are causing me alarm, a, a, a…"

## Activities – around 2 years
- Let finger 'ants' crawl up arms and legs while saying a-a-a.
- Play-dough ants marching across the table.

## Activities – around 4 years
- Find /a/ at the start of words: apple, ant, astronaut.
- Trace and write 'a', starting at the top like a 'c'.

## Blending words
Oral blending: **a-t** → at (once /t/ is known), **s-a-t** → sat.
