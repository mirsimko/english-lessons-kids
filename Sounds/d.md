# /d/

← [[m]] · [[Group 2]]

## Story
A drummer beats a drum in a parade: d, d, d.

## Action
Beat your hands up and down as if playing a drum, saying d, d, d.

## Song idea
Sing "Beat the drum, d, d, d…"

## Activities – around 2 years
- Drum on an upturned pot with wooden spoons.
- March in a parade around the room.

## Activities – around 4 years
- Find /d/: dog, duck, door.
- Write 'd': round like 'c', then up tall and down.

## Blending words
**d-a-d** → dad, **r-e-d** → red, **d-i-p** → dip, **m-a-d** → mad, **s-a-d** → sad, **h-i-d** → hid.
