# /c / k/

[[Group 2]] · [[e]] →

## Story
A child clicks castanets in time to music: c, c, c.

## Action
Raise your hands and click your fingers like castanets, saying c, c, c.

## Song idea
Sing "Click the castanets, c, c, c…"

## Activities – around 2 years
- Click fingers or tap two spoons together.
- Find cat and car toys and click for each.

## Activities – around 4 years
- I spy /c/: cup, car, cat. Explain that 'c' and 'k' make the same sound.
- Write 'c' (like starting an 'a') and 'k'.

## Blending words
**c-a-t** → cat, **c-a-p** → cap, **c-a-n** → can, **k-i-t** → kit, **k-i-d** (after /d/).
