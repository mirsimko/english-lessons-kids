# /p/

← [[i]] · [[Group 1]] · [[n]] →

## Story
Birthday candles on a cake are puffed out: p, p, p.

## Action
Hold up a finger like a candle and puff it out, saying p, p, p.

## Song idea
Sing "Puff out the candles, p, p, p…"

## Activities – around 2 years
- Blow out real or pretend candles, cotton-wool balls, or bubbles.
- Puff a feather across the table.

## Activities – around 4 years
- I spy /p/: pen, pig, pot.
- Write 'p': down, then up and round.

## Blending words
**p-a-t** → pat, **t-a-p** → tap, **p-i-t** → pit, **s-i-p** → sip, **t-i-p** → tip.
