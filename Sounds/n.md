# /n/

← [[p]] · [[Group 1]]

## Story
A plane flies overhead with its engine going nnnnn.

## Action
Hold your arms out like a plane's wings and 'fly', saying nnnnn.

## Song idea
Sing "Hear the aeroplane, nnnn…"

## Activities – around 2 years
- Zoom around the room as planes, humming nnnn.
- Fly a toy plane over a big 'n' drawn in chalk.

## Activities – around 4 years
- Find /n/: net, nose, nut.
- Write 'n': down, up and over.

## Blending words
**n-a-p** → nap, **p-i-n** → pin, **t-i-n** → tin, **p-a-n** → pan, **a-n-t** → ant, **s-n-a-p** → snap.
