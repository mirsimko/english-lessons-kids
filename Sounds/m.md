# /m/

← [[r]] · [[Group 2]] · [[d]] →

## Story
Looking at a yummy meal and rubbing tummies: mmm.

## Action
Rub your tummy while saying mmmmm.

## Song idea
Sing "Mmm, mmm, the food is yummy…"

## Activities – around 2 years
- Pretend picnic – rub tummies after every 'bite'.
- Play-dough food tasting.

## Activities – around 4 years
- I spy /m/: mug, map, mat.
- Write 'm': down, up and over, up and over.

## Blending words
**m-a-t** → mat, **m-a-n** → man, **m-a-p** → map, **m-e-t** → met, **h-a-m** → ham.
